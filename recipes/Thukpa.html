Thukpa Recipes

Description

Thukpa is one of the very popular dish in Nepal.

Ingredients

  • noodles - 200gm
  • Pork (small pieces) - 100 gm
  • Pork bones - 100gm
  • Onion(julien) - 50 gm
  • Soya sauce - 10 ml
  • Carrot - 10 gm
  • Chilli - 5 gm
  • Msg - 5 gm
  • Salt - 5 gm
  • Oil - 7 ml
  • water 250ml
  • lemon or lime - 2
  1. Boil the noodles in 200 ml water for 4-5 minutes
  2. Take out the noodles out of the pot and let it drain the water for 30 minutes to dry.
  3. Heat the pot put oil and heat it for 2 minutes.
  4. Fry the pork pieces until it gets crisp.
  5. Put all the other vegetables and bones into the pot and fry for 3 minutes
  6. Put the msg and salt into the pot.
  7. Pour the water into the pot, close the lid and boil it for 30 minutes.
  8. Now make a serving of noodles in the bowl.
  9. Pour the soup and ingredients into the bowl of noodles
  10. The Thukpa is ready. Enjoy
