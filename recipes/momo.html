MoMo Recipes

Description

MOMO is the very popular dish in Nepal.

Ingredients

  • Wheat Flour - 1 kg
  • Pork Keema - 500 gm
  • Onion(finely chopped) - 500 gm
  • Ginger(paste) - 20 gm
  • Msg - 10 gm
  • Salt - 10 gm
  • Oil - 20 ml
  • Scallion - 100 gm
  1. Make a dough out of wheat flour and put it in the refrigerator for 15 minutes
  2. Mix all other the ingredients togethre very well
  3. Make ready a momo steaming pot and boil water.
  4. Make small roti out of the dough with rolling pin
  5. Fill the ingredients in the small roti and wrap it
  6. Now fill the momo steaming basket and put the basket inside the steaming pot and wait for 15 minutes.
  7. After 15 minutes your momo is ready to eat. Ramrari Khanus la. Enjoy your momo.
