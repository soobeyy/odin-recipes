Chowmein Recipes

Description

Chowmein is the very popular dish in Nepal.

Ingredients

  • Chowmein noodles - 200gm
  • Pork - 100 gm
  • Onion(julien) - 50 gm
  • Soya sauce - 10 ml
  • Carrot - 10 gm
  • Bell pepper - 10 gm
  • Chilli - 5 gm
  • Msg - 5 gm
  • Salt - 5 gm
  • Oil - 7 ml
  • Scallion - 50 gm
  1. Boil the Chowmein noodles in 200 ml water for 4-5 minutes
  2. Take out the noodles out of the pot and let it drain the water for 30 minutes to dry.
  3. Heat the Wok put oil.
  4. Fry the pork pieces until it gets crisp.
  5. Put all other vegetables into the wok and fry for 3 minutes
  6. Put the noodles into the wok and mix together with the ingredients.
  7. Put the soya sauce, msg and salt together and mix it for 2-3 minutes in high heat.
  8. Turn off the stove and it's ready to serve. Enjoy
